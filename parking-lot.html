Parking Lot FAQs:

How do I know if my parking lot needs repairs?

Common signs include cracks, potholes, pooling water, faded striping, crumbling edges, and uneven pavement. Repairing these issues early can help prevent more extensive and costly damage later.

How often should a parking lot be sealcoated?

Most parking lots should be sealcoated every 2–3 years, depending on traffic, weather, and wear. Sealcoating helps protect the asphalt from water, UV rays, oil spills, and cracking, extending the life of your pavement.

Can you repair a parking lot without replacing the entire surface?

Yes. In many cases, cracks, potholes, drainage problems, and worn areas can be repaired without a full replacement. Services such as crack filling, patching, resurfacing, and sealcoating can restore the parking lot and improve its appearance at a lower cost.
