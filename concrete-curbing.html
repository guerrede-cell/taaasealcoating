Concrete & Curbing FAQs:

How long does new concrete take to dry and cure?

Concrete typically sets within 24–48 hours, but it takes about 7 days to support normal use and up to 28 days to fully cure and reach maximum strength

What are the benefits of installing concrete curbing?

Concrete curbing helps define landscaping, parking lots, and driveways while improving appearance and preventing erosion. It also helps keep mulch, gravel, and soil in place and creates a clean, finished look.

Can cracked or damaged concrete be repaired?

Yes. Small cracks and minor surface damage can often be repaired with patching or resurfacing.  More severe damage, such as large cracks, sinking, or crumbling sections, may require partial or full replacement.
