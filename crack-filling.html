Crack Filling FAQs:

What is crack filling and why is it important?

Crack filling is the process of sealing cracks in asphalt to prevent water, dirt, and debris from getting underneath the pavement. Filling cracks early helps prevent larger problems like potholes, erosion, and costly repairs.

When should cracks in asphalt be filled?

Cracks should be filled as soon as they appear, especially before winter or rainy seasons. Water can enter the cracks and expand when temperatures change, causing the damage to get worse.

How large of a crack can be repaired with crack filling?

Crack filling is usually recommended for cracks up to about 1 inch wide. Larger cracks or areas with severe damage may require patching or more extensive repairs.
