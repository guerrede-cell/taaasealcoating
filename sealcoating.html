Sealcoating FAQs:

How often should asphalt be sealcoated?

Most driveways and parking lots should be sealcoated every 2–3 years, depending on traffic, weather, and wear. Regular sealcoating helps protect the surface and extend the life of the asphalt.

How long does sealcoating take to dry?

Sealcoating usually dries enough for foot traffic within 4–6 hours and for vehicles within 24–48 hours. Drying time can vary based on temperature, humidity, and weather conditions.

Can sealcoating fix cracks and potholes?

No. Sealcoating is meant to protect the surface, not repair major damage. Cracks and potholes should be repaired before sealcoating to ensure the best results.
